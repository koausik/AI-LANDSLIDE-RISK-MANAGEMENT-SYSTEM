What We Built: The Aegis Solution

Traditional warning systems rely on delayed ground rain gauges that fail to predict sudden debris flows. We designed an end-to-end telemetry pipeline that ingests spaceborne radar and models geotechnical limit equilibrium in seconds.

🛰️

Multi-Spectrum Space Telemetry

Automated real-time ingestion from Copernicus & Open-Meteo feeds. Gathers hourly 24h precipitation accumulations ($P$) and volumetric soil moisture index ($m_s$) at root-zone pore level.

Zero Ground Hardware Required
🧠

Ensemble Geotechnical AI

A Random Forest classifier trained on thousands of simulated geotechnical slope variations. Evaluates the physical coupling between gravity shear stress, slope angle ($\theta$), and hydrostatic pore pressures.

Continuous Probability Calibration
🚨

Early Hazard Triage & Alert Engine

Instantly calculates Factor of Safety and failure probability. Automatically flags sectors into Safe, Watch, or Critical states with evacuation guidance tailored for SDRF/NDRF emergency response.
